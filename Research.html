Human Life Preferences and Personality Assessment Study

A quantitative analysis of value priorities under resource constraints

🎯 Research Framework

Research Title:

"An Empirical Study of Life Value Preferences and Their Correlation with Age and Gender: A Resource Allocation Perspective"

Research Questions:

  • What are the primary life value preferences among individuals when faced with resource constraints?
  • How does age influence the prioritization of different life values?
  • Are there significant gender differences in life value preferences?
  • What personality patterns emerge from value allocation choices?

🔬 Research Hypotheses:

H1: There is a significant positive correlation between age and health preference (older individuals prioritize health more)

H2: Males and females show significantly different patterns in beauty and social acceptance preferences

H3: Intelligence and creativity preferences are positively correlated

H4: Wealth preference decreases with age as individuals mature

    🎓 Research Conclusions & Implications

    Limitations:

    • Small sample size (n=17) limits generalizability
    • Age distribution skewed towards younger participants
    • Self-reported data may contain response bias
    • Cross-sectional design limits causal inferences

    Future Research Directions:

    • Expand sample size and age range
    • Include cultural and socioeconomic variables
    • Longitudinal study to track preference changes over time
    • Experimental validation of resource allocation decisions
